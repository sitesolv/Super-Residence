Hotel Pool

Set in the beautiful countryside of Sri Lanka, Super Residence is a peaceful place to relax and enjoy nature. Our villa offers modern comfort and a quiet escape from the busy world.

You can relax by our lovely pool, surrounded by green gardens, or take a refreshing swim. We are close to stunning beaches, so you can enjoy both the pool and the sea.

At Super Residence, we work hard to make sure your stay is relaxing and special.

Culinary Experience

Restaurant & Dining

Savor authentic Ceylon flavors, ocean-fresh seafood & tropical delicacies.

Immerse your senses in a delightful gastronomic journey at Super Residence. Our in-house dining celebrates the rich culinary heritage of Sri Lanka, pairing aromatic local spices with the freshest catch sourced daily from southern coast fishermen.

From wholesome tropical breakfast spreads on the breezy balcony to leisurely poolside snacks and intimate candlelight seafood dinners under the stars, every dish is prepared with passion, freshness, and warm Sri Lankan hospitality.
